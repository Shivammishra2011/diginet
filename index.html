About FoundationPro

A small, focused coaching center built on one simple belief — every child can excel with the right guidance and genuine care.

Learning that feels personal, not mechanical

FoundationPro was started in 2012 with a simple idea: instead of large, crowded classrooms where students get lost, we would keep our batches small and our teaching deeply personal. Today, we continue that promise with just two dedicated teachers who know every student by name.

We teach students from Class 1 to Class 12, with a special emphasis on foundation building in the early years. Whether your child needs extra support, wants to stay ahead, or is preparing for competitive exams, we design the learning around them — not the other way around.

Our online and offline modes mean families can choose what fits their routine best. And because we believe in transparency, we offer a free demo class before any commitment.

  • Small batches — maximum 8 students per batch for real attention.
  • Personal mentoring — direct access to both teachers, not just a helpdesk.
  • Regular parent updates — because progress is a team effort.
  • No hidden fees — clear, honest pricing with flexible plans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions parents ask us most.

Do you offer both online and offline classes?

Yes! We offer both online (via Zoom/Google Meet) and offline classes at our center in Sector 15, New Delhi. You can choose whichever mode suits your family's schedule, or even switch between them.

What is the batch size?

We keep our batches intentionally small — a maximum of 8 students per batch. This ensures every child gets personal attention and can ask questions freely without hesitation.

Is there a free demo class?

Absolutely. We offer one free demo class so you and your child can experience our teaching style before enrolling. No payment or commitment is required for the demo.

Which classes and subjects do you cover?

We cover Class 1 to Class 12, with a strong focus on foundation building. Subjects include Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Studies. We also offer Olympiad and NTSE foundation classes for interested students.

How do you track student progress?

We conduct regular chapter tests and monthly assessments. Detailed progress reports are shared with parents every month, and we're always available for a quick call if you have concerns.

What are your fees?

Fees vary by class and subject combination. We keep our pricing transparent and affordable, with flexible monthly plans and sibling discounts. Please contact us on WhatsApp or call us for a detailed fee structure.

Can I change my batch timing later?

Yes, subject to availability. We understand that schedules change, so we try our best to accommodate timing changes. Just let us know in advance and we'll find a slot that works for you.
